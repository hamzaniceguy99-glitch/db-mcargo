What we provide

DB-M CARGO provides road freight transport and warehousing services provided by DB-M Cargo, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0840.029.106. Road haulage for hire or reward using vehicles over 3.5 tonnes requires access to the occupation under Regulations (EC) 1071/2009 and 1072/2009: a community licence, a designated transport manager holding the certificate of professional competence, good repute and proof of financial standing. The company holds the community licence under [COMMUNITY LICENCE NUMBER] and its transport manager holds the certificate of professional competence under [CPC HOLDER AND CERTIFICATE NUMBER]. IMPORTANT — LIMITS OF LIABILITY: carriage of goods by road between countries is governed by the CMR Convention, which limits the carrier's compensation for loss of or damage to goods to 8.33 special drawing rights per kilogram of gross weight short, irrespective of the commercial value of the consignment, together with the carriage charges. Domestic carriage is subject to the limits applicable under Belgian law and to the agreed conditions. A shipper whose goods are worth more than that limit should arrange ad valorem or goods-in-transit insurance before the consignment moves, and the company says so in writing when quoting rather than after a claim. Claims are subject to the notice periods and limitation periods the CMR Convention prescribes. Driving and rest times are governed by Regulation (EC) 561/2006 and recorded by digital tachograph; the company does not accept a collection or delivery schedule that could only be met by exceeding them, and a requested time slot is therefore sometimes refused. Cabotage is performed only within the limits Regulation (EC) 1072/2009 allows. THE COMPANY DOES NOT CARRY DANGEROUS GOODS within the meaning of the ADR Agreement, and any consignment found to contain them is refused. Goods requiring temperature control under food hygiene rules are not accepted, the company holding no authorisation from the Federal Agency for the Safety of the Food Chain for that purpose. Warehousing is provided on the conditions agreed in writing, including insured value and the limits of the depositary's liability. NO TRANSIT TIME IS GUARANTEED where traffic, weather, border formalities, customs inspection or a consignee's own receiving hours delay a vehicle. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
